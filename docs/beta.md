# Beta with guitarists

A one-week beta of the live site with 3–5 guitarists of mixed levels, before v1.0.

- Live site: <https://ateliersix.netlify.app>
- Feedback form (about two minutes):
  <https://github.com/WIPNHPT1/atelier-six/issues/new?template=beta_feedback.yml>
- Scores are out of 5 for **playable**, **sounds like the style** and **fun**. Anything
  scoring under 4 gets reworked, the same rule as the review gate.
